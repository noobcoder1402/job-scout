# My Job Criteria

## What I'm Looking For

Describe your background and what kind of roles you want. The AI matcher uses this to score every job it finds.

## Role Types (in order of preference)

1. Marketing / Growth / GTM
2. Product Management
3. Strategy / Operations
4. Any other role type you're interested in

## Must-Haves

- Industry or company type (e.g. B2B SaaS)
- Seniority level (e.g. Manager and above, 5+ years experience)
- Location preferences (e.g. remote-friendly, based in a specific city)

## Hard No

- Role types you never want to see (e.g. pure sales roles)
- Location restrictions that don't work for you
