# My Job Criteria

## What I'm Looking For

Senior product manager looking to lead roadmap at a B2B developer-tools or infrastructure company. Business-facing roles on technically complex products — I don't write code myself.

## Role Types (in order of preference)

1. **Product Management** — Senior PM, Group PM, Director of Product
2. **Product Strategy** — Chief of Staff to CPO, Head of Product Ops
3. **Technical Program Management** — only if product-adjacent (owns outcomes, not just timelines)
4. **Developer Relations** — only if it includes product ownership, not purely content or community

## Must-Haves

- B2B or developer-tools company (not consumer, not adtech, not e-commerce)
- Senior enough to own a roadmap — at least Senior PM level or equivalent
- Remote-friendly or based in London (open to hybrid for the right role)

## Nice-to-Haves (push the score higher)

- Company is Series B+ or public — past the chaos stage
- Role sits close to engineering leadership, not buried in a shared-services team
- Job description mentions API products, platform, or developer experience
- Team size of 5+ reports to the role, or it's a high-leverage IC with direct CEO/CPO access

## Hard No

- Pure engineering, data science, or design roles — I'm not a developer
- Companies with fewer than 20 employees — too early-stage for what I want
- Consulting, staffing, or agency roles
- Entry-level or mid-level roles (Associate PM, PM I/II without "Senior" or "Lead")
- Roles that explicitly require US work authorization (e.g. "must be located in the US", "US-based only"). Roles that list a US city as one of several locations are fine — only exclude if US residency is a hard requirement
